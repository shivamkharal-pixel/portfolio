About Me

My Story

My name is Shivam Kharal, and I come from Karnali Province, one of the most remote regions of Nepal. Growing up in a farming family, I studied in a small government school because attending a private school was beyond my family's means. Despite limited resources, I always carried big dreams and a strong determination to create a better future.

From a young age, I believed that education could change my life. Every challenge I faced became a source of motivation rather than an obstacle. Today, I am passionately learning technology and working toward my dream of becoming an AI and Machine Learning Engineer who can build solutions that make a positive impact on society.

Beyond academics, I actively participate in extracurricular activities, leadership programs, and community initiatives. I enjoy learning new skills, exploring innovation, and helping others grow. My journey is a story of resilience, ambition, and hard work—a journey driven by the belief that no dream is too big for someone willing to work for it.
